# Shipping

## Shipping times
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2
business days and is available at checkout for an additional fee.

## Shipping addresses
You can ship to any address in the countries listed at checkout. PO boxes
are supported for standard shipping only, not express.

## Lost or delayed packages
If a package is marked as delivered but hasn't arrived, wait 48 hours
(carriers sometimes scan early), then contact support with the order ID.
