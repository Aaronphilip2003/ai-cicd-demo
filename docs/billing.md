# Billing

## Payment methods
We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. Payment methods can
be managed from Settings > Billing > Payment Methods.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5-10 business
days to appear, depending on your bank.

## Invoices
Invoices for every order are available under Settings > Billing >
Invoice History, and can be downloaded as PDF.
